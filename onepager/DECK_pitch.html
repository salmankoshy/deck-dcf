Deckers Outdoor (NYSE: DECK)  |  BUY  |  DCF value $131  |  Price $80.23 (Sep 21 2026)  |  Upside +63%
DECK trades at 7.1x EV/LTM EBITDA with zero debt. Management's FY27 guide is net sales of $5.86B to $5.91B.

Thesis at $80.23

  1. Cheapest multiple in the peer set. On stockanalysis numbers DECK trades at 7.4x EV/EBITDA against an 11.4x peer median. On GuruFocus numbers it trades at 7.6x against its own 13.5x ten-year median. The FY28 to FY30 framework calls for high-single-digit sales growth and operating margin in the low 20s.
  2. FY27 at guidance, out-years below the framework. FY27 growth of 7.5% equals the guide midpoint. FY29 and FY30 growth of 6% and 5% runs under the high-single-digit framework. Margin holds at 21.5% against a Jul 23 guide of slightly better.
  3. Cash generation funds the buyback. FY26 free cash flow topped $1B and DECK retired 10.5M shares for $1.075B (8-K May 21 2026). HOKA grew +7.7% in Q1 FY27 with DTC +17% (10-Q). Zero debt.

Valuation: four methods

Four methods land above the price. The perpetuity DCF gives $131 and the 9.5x exit multiple gives $117. Comps at the peer median give $122. The Monte Carlo P10 to P90 band runs $112 to $144. The price implies a 12.6% EBIT margin, held flat from FY27, against the 21.5% forecast. The perpetuity value implies a 104% return on new capital, close to FY26's 100.5% ROIC. At 25% it is $124.

Risks: HOKA growth and tariffs

  1. HOKA growth is decelerating. HOKA net sales grew +23.6% in FY25 and +15.9% in FY26, per the segment note in the FY2026 10-K. The Q1 FY27 10-Q shows +7.7%.
  2. Tariff rate went from 10% to 12.5%. On the Jul 23 2026 Q1 FY27 call, per SGI Europe coverage, the go-forward tariff rate moved to 12.5% from 10%.

Catalysts: Q2 FY27 and holiday quarter

The Q2 FY27 report will show whether HOKA DTC growth holds after the +17% Q1 print (10-Q). Then the holiday quarter, UGG's seasonal peak. The EPS guide assumes buybacks of about 80% of FY27 free cash flow.
